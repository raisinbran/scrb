## Overview

The meeting focused on finalizing the design specifications for a new remote control project. Speaker B presented research on components, leading to tentative agreements on kinetic energy sources and rubber casings. The team debated interface designs, ultimately deciding on a command interface while considering fruit and vegetable themes. Discussions covered material choices, button functions, and power systems, with several decisions made regarding the chip type and display exclusion. Action items were assigned to specific members for further research and design work before the next session.

## Main topics

### Power Source and Energy Systems

The group discussed various energy options for the remote control, including batteries, solar panels, and kinetic energy. After reviewing the feasibility of different systems, the team tentatively agreed to utilize a kinetic energy source to power the device, eliminating the need for external batteries or solar panels. This decision was made to prioritize innovation and reduce reliance on traditional power methods. However, concerns were raised regarding whether users would shake the remote sufficiently to generate adequate power. Consequently, the team tentatively agreed to investigate the possibility of incorporating a backup battery or solar power system to ensure the remote functions reliably when not being actively shaken, though this remains an open topic for further decision-making.

### Interface Design and User Interaction

The team evaluated different interface options, weighing graphical elements against command-based interactions. They reviewed fashion trends and market research suggesting fruit and vegetable themes, debating their applicability to a remote control. Ultimately, the group decided to use a command interface because it is simpler and more user-friendly, which also helps reduce design costs. While they tentatively agreed to incorporate graphical user interface points like symbols on buttons to enhance the design, they also tentatively agreed to limit the buttons to basic functions such as power, channel selection, and volume. The design must remain easy to use and accommodate a light-emitting diode for visual feedback, while excluding an LCD display due to budget constraints.

### Materials, Casing, and Aesthetic Themes

Discussions centered on the physical construction and visual appeal of the remote control. The team tentatively agreed that the exterior casing would be made of hard plastic with a rubber coating for protection and aesthetics, though this may complicate the design of interchangeable plates. Regarding the theme, the group debated literal representations of fruits and vegetables versus abstract shapes. They tentatively agreed to tone down the concept by moving away from literal images toward a more abstract approach, while also considering international tastes and the 15 to 25 year old age market. The buttons were tentatively agreed to be black to allow for a lighting-up effect, possibly using translucent rubber, maintaining a simplistic look with all buttons the same color.
