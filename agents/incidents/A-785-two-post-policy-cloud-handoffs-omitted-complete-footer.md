### A-785 — 2026-11-18 — Post-policy cloud closeouts omitted the complete mandatory reporting footer

- **Status / attribution:** Confirmed reporting-procedure failure in two later non-Claude owner-facing handoffs (PR #362 User Manual implementation; PR #363 PR #352 CI/review closeout), discovered by an independent records-and-response audit. These are **not** application, UI-test, or CI implementation defects.
- **What failed:** Both handoffs included useful accounting summaries and linked new 12-column usage rows, but neither gave the required complete owner-visible footer with separate start/end five-hour and weekly balances (or specific unavailable reasons), reset/overlap information, and all task start/end and retry/disposition fields. Having a usage file or PR description is not equivalent to reporting its required contents at task completion. The corrected row files do not retroactively change the original message.
- **Source evidence:** The two original owner-facing completions available to the current audit, [PR #362](https://github.com/raisinbran/scrb/pull/362)'s branch row `agents/usage-log/2026-11-18-v1-offline-manual-implementation.md`, [PR #363](https://github.com/raisinbran/scrb/pull/363)'s two usage rows and handoff, and [issue #358](https://github.com/raisinbran/scrb/issues/358) policy. Sanitized audit findings are in `docs/handoffs/2026-11-18-reporting-compliance-incident-reconciliation.md`; no raw session/private telemetry is published.
- **Cause:** Completion messages summarized quota availability and task outcomes without checking every field of the non-Claude mandatory closeout template, even though source records had more context. Exact actual meter values and some historical task boundaries were not accessible; that does not justify omitting the explicit source-specific unavailability fields.
- **Impact:** Two independently sampled post-policy handoffs are **REPORTING NONCOMPLIANT** even though both produced 12-column rows. Owner follow-up and audit reconciliation were required. Actual task-attributable quota usage, incremental cost, and owner time are not measured.
- **Correction:** Preserve original rows, source commits, comments and prior messages; publish an audit ledger and this distinct incident on a reviewed documentation PR. New handoffs must include complete explicit PT start/end, model/effort or source-specific unavailable reason, 5h/weekly start/end or reason, resets, overlap, retries, row's publication status and incident references. Do not invent historical times or use another account's quota.
- **Relation to A-771:** Mainline A-771 documents the earlier recurring Mac/Regiurn coordination deficiency; A-785 documents **two separately observed subsequent cloud handoffs after the clarified policy**. Neither supersedes nor erases the other.
- **Remaining risk:** Future compliance remains unverified until actual complete cloud and desktop handoffs are sampled and their rows reviewed; local Mac/Regiurn incident/usage files cannot be authenticated from this cloud connection. Issue #358 must stay OPEN.
