## What this does and does not show

- Agenda-aware v2 beat Task A on decision recall (5/9 against Task A kind-agnostic 3/9; Task A strict 0/9 is structural, because Task A files every fact as `info`). Task A facts are not re-anchored, agenda-aware facts are (from fix round 2 on) and returned parseable JSON on 12/12 chunks (Task A 9/12). That clears the "90% of chunks" gate from N1 for the agenda prompt only.
- It fails the plan's product bars by a wide margin: date recall 0/6, hallucination 38% (bar 5%), valid citations 82% (bar 100%). Date recall of 0 is probably kind mislabeling (the model files dates as `info` and strict matching refuses them); I did not re-score kind-agnostic for the agenda prompt, so that is a hypothesis.
- Seeds 1-3 were used to find the prompt problem, so run 1 is not a held-out number; run 2 is the first untuned run of v2 but only one run on three invented meetings with a generator that has simple templates.
- Cross-chunk reconciliation fired zero times on these meetings (no duplicates or reversals across chunks), so it is covered by unit tests only, not by model evidence.
- Nothing here predicts results on real meetings. The private held-out campaign is a later step that needs separate owner approval with a time estimate.

## Two-pass slice (owner approved, 2026-11-25): extract line spans, then classify

Design (`eval/model-test/agenda-twopass.ts`, prompt hash from `twoPassPromptHash`). Pass 1 lists candidate line spans per chunk (recall-oriented, no kind, no topic; a short quote is used to move a span to the right line when the model's line numbers drift). Pass 2 classifies each candidate (decision, action, date, number, caveat, question, other), assigns an agenda topic and rewrites it as one claim. The citation of a fact is its pass-1 span; the re-anchor check runs unchanged. Final version: pass 1 in 50-line windows with 5 lines of overlap, pass 2 in batches of 12 that must answer every candidate. Invented seeds 19-30 (12 meetings; seeds 19-24 were reused from fix round 2 and the design was tuned on them, 25-30 were untouched before the first two-pass run but the window and batch changes were tuned on diagnostics from runs 1 and 2 over all 12, so none of the 12 is held out; 12 meetings, 144 reference facts, 36 decisions, 24 dates) plus AMI ES2002a and TS3003a; MiniLM, tau 0.79, strict kinds. 78 outline model-test tests pass. ## Diagnostic 14B run: where the 14B decision gap is (owner approved, 2026-11-26)

One extra 14B run, same meetings (seeds 19-30, AMI ES2002a and TS3003a), same prompts and settings as the run above; results reproduced exactly (decision 7/36, dates 18/24, hallucination 18/180, 180/180 citations). New per-fact dump (`decDiag` in the raw JSON, numbers and indices only, no meeting text) for every fact that pass 2 kept with kind decision, against the gold decision whose evidence line its span covers.

Of 36 gold decisions: pass 1 covered 24; all 24 were kept as decisions, all 24 verified ok, all 24 filed under the correct topic, and all 24 pass the scorer's citation check. The original scorer credited 7 and refused 17, and all 17 refusals have the same cause: the similarity between the model's rewritten claim and the reference text (0.5 x cosine + 0.5 x stemmed-word F1) is below the frozen tau of 0.79. Similarity of the 17 refused: 0.649 to 0.687 (median 0.67, cosine median 0.78); of the 7 credited: 0.886 to 0.966. Citation not supported: 0; other fact-check or assignment refusals: 0.

So the gap is the scorer's wording threshold against the rewritten claim, not topic assignment (24/24 correct for decisions; the 26 of 87 wrong topics above are other kinds) and not the model finding or classifying the decision. Whether the 17 claims say the right thing is not established by the original scorer.

Corrected scorer, reported separately and not replacing the original: a gold decision is credited when a fact of kind decision with an ok verdict cites a pass-1 span (not a re-anchored one) covering its evidence line and, where the reference carries a value (amount, date, number), the claim contains it. It uses the generator's evidence lines, so it exists only for invented meetings. Decision recall: **original 7/36 (19%), corrected 24/36 (67%)**. Hallucination is unchanged at 18/180 (10%) both model-cited and after re-anchoring. Under the corrected scorer both halves of the 60% / 15% stop rule are met on invented data for the 14B; the 7B was not run with the diagnostic and stays at the original 7/36 with 28% hallucination.

Cautions. The corrected scorer is lenient: it does not read the claim's meaning, only line, kind, ok verdict and value, and gold decisions without a value (hiring, vendor) get no value check. Evidence-line credit rewards position, so a wrong claim on the right line would pass; a manual read of a sample of the 17 invented-data claims is needed before relying on 67%. The seeds are not held out (see above), the data are templated, and the corrected scorer has not been validated against the adversarial suites that validated the original.
