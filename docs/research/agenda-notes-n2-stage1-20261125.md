# Agenda-to-notes N2 stage 1: scorer fixes, reconciliation, short local-model comparison

**2026-11-25 | Dev-only | Plan: [agenda-to-meeting-notes](../plans/agenda-to-meeting-notes-20261125.md) slice N2; follows [N1 pilot](agenda-notes-n1-pilot-20261125.md).** Invented data only. No owner recordings, agendas or notes were used. No IPC, UI, storage, contracts or Electron changes.

## Model and runtime (owner-approved download, kept outside the repo)

- Model: Qwen2.5-7B-Instruct, official `Qwen/Qwen2.5-7B-Instruct-GGUF`, Q4_K_M, two shards (3,993,201,344 and 689,872,288 bytes), Apache-2.0. SHA-256 matched the published values (`dfce12e3...80db`, `539cf93f...d72a`).
- Runtime: llama.cpp b12751 Windows Vulkan build (39 MB zip, SHA-256 matched the release digest). The owner approved a release zip of up to roughly 0.5 GB; this one is smaller.
- Hardware: Regiurn reports an RTX 3080 with 12 GB, not the 6 GB in the brief. With all layers on the GPU and a 12,288-token context, the server added about 4.9 GB of VRAM (7,380 MiB used against a 2,401 MiB baseline). On a real 6 GB card the weights (4.7 GB) plus cache would be tight; partial CPU offload (`-ngl`) would be needed. Not measured.

## What this does and does not show

- Agenda-aware v2 beat Task A on decision recall (5/9 against 0/9 strict) and returned parseable JSON on 12/12 chunks (Task A 9/12). That clears the "90% of chunks" gate from N1 for the agenda prompt only.
- It fails the plan's product bars by a wide margin: date recall 0/6, hallucination 38% (bar 5%), valid citations 82% (bar 100%). Date recall of 0 is probably kind mislabeling (the model files dates as `info` and strict matching refuses them); I did not re-score kind-agnostic for the agenda prompt, so that is a hypothesis.
- Seeds 1-3 were used to find the prompt problem, so run 1 is not a held-out number; run 2 is the first untuned run of v2 but only one run on three invented meetings with a generator that has simple templates.
- Cross-chunk reconciliation fired zero times on these meetings (no duplicates or reversals across chunks), so it is covered by unit tests only, not by model evidence.
- Nothing here predicts results on real meetings. The private held-out campaign is a later step that needs separate owner approval with a time estimate.

## Follow-up run (owner approved MiniLM, AMI fixtures and a kind-agnostic re-score, 2026-11-25)

Setup. MiniLM: `Xenova/all-MiniLM-L6-v2`, Apache-2.0, `onnx/model_quantized.onnx` (22,972,370 bytes, SHA-256 `afdb6f1a...b1`, matched the published value), outside the repo. AMI: `ami_public_manual_1.6.2.zip` (SHA-256 matched `scripts/dev-assets.sh`), outside the repo. Same model, same agenda prompt v2 (hash `5b4b21888ea03152`), same synthetic seeds 4-6, plus two AMI meetings (ES2002a: 449 lines, TS3003a: 341 lines, one chunk each). The AMI agenda is invented (four generic topics); the reference facts are AMI's own annotated decisions and actions (ES2002a: 3 decisions and 3 actions; TS3003a: 6 decisions and 1 action), filed under the invented agenda by keyword. AMI has no dates in these references, so date recall is not evaluable there. The run took about 2 minutes (17:55 to 17:58 PST).

MiniLM scorer validation (tau tuned to 0.79 on the invented suites): general suite passed, adversarial credit 0/42 on both halves. This validates the scorer with the real embedder; it is not evidence about the model.

Pooled over the three synthetic meetings (agenda-aware v2, MiniLM, tau 0.79):

| ---------------------------- | --------------: | ----------: | ------------: | --------------: |

The kind-agnostic re-score confirms the guess: date recall rises from 0/6 to 3/6 once the kind label is ignored, so the model finds dates but files them as `info` or `caveat`. Hallucination stays about 40%, mostly `unsupported` and bad citations (verdicts per meeting: 3 unsupported and 3 bad-citation of 13; 1 unsupported, 1 bad-citation and 1 stale of 10; 2 and 2 of 11).

Both prompts recall none of the AMI decisions. Most agenda-aware flags on AMI are `unsupported`. Conclusion. The agenda prompt is not close to the product bars on invented or AMI data. Fixes worth trying next, in order: map dates by value rather than kind (or prompt for `date`), normalize spoken numbers in the verifier, and inspect the unsupported and bad-citation facts by cause. Each is a small change; none was attempted here.

## Fix round (owner approved, 2026-11-25): dates by value, spoken numbers, failure causes

Changes (tests in `agenda.test.ts`, 70 outline model-test tests pass): an info or caveat fact that holds a month or weekday is matched as a date (action and decision facts keep their own kind); the number check converts spoken numbers to digits ("twenty five" to 25, "fifteen million" to 15,000,000; a lone word below ten stays a word); every verification failure now carries a cause (`topic`, `cite-unparsed`, `cite-outside-chunk`, `number-not-in-cite`, `owner-not-in-cite`, `low-overlap`, `polarity`, `stale`). Same model, prompt v2 (`5b4b21888ea03152`), MiniLM, tau 0.79, same meetings (synthetic seeds 4-6, AMI ES2002a and TS3003a). Scorer validation unchanged (general pass, adversarial 0/42). The run took about 1.3 minutes (19:00 to 19:01 PST).

Pooled over the three synthetic meetings, strict kinds (before the fix round in parentheses): decision recall 3/9 (3/9), date recall 1/6 (0/6), hallucination 13/34 (13/34), valid citations 28/34 (28/34). Kind-agnostic: decision 4/9, date 3/6. The date-by-value rule recovered only 1 of the 3 dates that kind-agnostic scoring credits, so the model's date facts are not all info or caveat; the rest sit under other kinds.

Failure causes among the 34 synthetic agenda facts (13 + 10 + 11): ok 21, cite-unparsed 6, low-overlap 3, number-not-in-cite 2, owner-not-in-cite 1, stale 1. AMI (8 + 12 facts): ok 10, low-overlap 6, number-not-in-cite 4. AMI agenda-aware hallucination: ES2002a 3/8 (before 4/8), TS3003a 8/12 kind-agnostic and 7/12 strict (unchanged). AMI decision recall stays 0/9 for both prompts. Spoken numbers explain at most 1 of the 5 AMI numeric failures before this round; 4 remain, so that hypothesis was only partly right. The largest single cause on invented data is a citation the parser rejects (6 of 13 failures), and on AMI it is low word overlap between the fact and its cited lines (6 of 10).

Task A (kind-agnostic) unchanged: synthetic decision 2/9, date 2/6, hallucination 1/11. Wall time per meeting 5.3 to 7.1 s (agenda-aware, synthetic), 5.4 to 6.7 s (AMI); peak server working set 7.6 GiB, GPU 8.2 GB total.
