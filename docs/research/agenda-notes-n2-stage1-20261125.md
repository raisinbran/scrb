**2026-11-25 | Dev-only | Plan: [agenda-to-meeting-notes](../plans/agenda-to-meeting-notes-20261125.md) slice N2; follows [N1 pilot](agenda-notes-n1-pilot-20261125.md).** Invented data only. No owner recordings, agendas or notes were used. No IPC, UI, storage, contracts or Electron changes.

## Model and runtime (owner-approved download, kept outside the repo)

- Model: Qwen2.5-7B-Instruct, official `Qwen/Qwen2.5-7B-Instruct-GGUF`, Q4_K_M, two shards (3,993,201,344 and 689,872,288 bytes), Apache-2.0. SHA-256 matched the published values (`dfce12e3...80db`, `539cf93f...d72a`).
- Runtime: llama.cpp b12751 Windows Vulkan build (39 MB zip, SHA-256 matched the release digest). The owner approved a release zip of up to roughly 0.5 GB; this one is smaller.
- Hardware: Regiurn reports an RTX 3080 with 12 GB, not the 6 GB in the brief. With all layers on the GPU and a 12,288-token context, the server added about 4.9 GB of VRAM (7,380 MiB used against a 2,401 MiB baseline). On a real 6 GB card the weights (4.7 GB) plus cache would be tight; partial CPU offload (`-ngl`) would be needed. Not measured.

## What this does and does not show

- Agenda-aware v2 beat Task A on decision recall (5/9 against 0/9 strict) and returned parseable JSON on 12/12 chunks (Task A 9/12). That clears the "90% of chunks" gate from N1 for the agenda prompt only.
- It fails the plan's product bars by a wide margin: date recall 0/6, hallucination 38% (bar 5%), valid citations 82% (bar 100%). Date recall of 0 is probably kind mislabeling (the model files dates as `info` and strict matching refuses them); I did not re-score kind-agnostic for the agenda prompt, so that is a hypothesis.
- Seeds 1-3 were used to find the prompt problem, so run 1 is not a held-out number; run 2 is the first untuned run of v2 but only one run on three invented meetings with a generator that has simple templates.
- Cross-chunk reconciliation fired zero times on these meetings (no duplicates or reversals across chunks), so it is covered by unit tests only, not by model evidence.
- Nothing here predicts results on real meetings. The private held-out campaign is a later step that needs separate owner approval with a time estimate.

## Fix round (owner approved, 2026-11-25): dates by value, spoken numbers, failure causes

## Citation formats and prompt tightening (owner approved, 2026-11-25)

Citation strings the 7B model produced, counted from the raw output of one run (prompt v2, invented seeds 4-6 and AMI ES2002a, TS3003a; line numbers only, no transcript text):

| Format (digits shown as N)                                             | Agenda prompt v2 |                                              Parsed |
| ---------------------------------------------------------------------- | ---------------: | --------------------------------------------------: |
| `LN-LN`                                                                |               27 |                                                  27 |
| `LN`                                                                   |               22 | 21 (one `L233` in a 228-line meeting, out of range) |
| Comma list (`LN, LN, ...`, 3 to 10 entries, one starting with a range) |                5 |                                                   0 |
| Task A prompt, `LN-LN` and `LN`                                        |               15 |                                                  15 |

That is 6 rejected of 54, which are the 6 unparsed citations. The comma lists were evenly spaced lines spread across the whole transcript for a single fact, not a contiguous range, so the output is not consistently valid and the parser was left alone. The prompt now asks for exactly one contiguous range of at most 6 lines, never a list, copied from the line markers (agenda prompt hash `0d9afaeb791be7ab`, was `5b4b21888ea03152`).

Re-run once, same model, MiniLM, tau 0.79, same meetings (seeds 4-6 were used to find the problem, so these are not held out). Pooled synthetic, strict kinds, with the v2 fix-round result in parentheses: decision recall 1/9 (3/9), date recall 2/6 (1/6), hallucination 7/25 (13/34), valid citations 21/25 (28/34). Kind-agnostic: decision 3/9, date 3/6. Facts returned: synthetic 25 (34), AMI 8 (20). Citation formats after the change: `LN-LN` 16 of 16 parsed, `LN` 14 of 14, comma lists 4 of 4 rejected (two of them 60 to 90 entries long, degenerate repetition), 34 in all. Failure causes, synthetic: ok 18, cite-unparsed 4, owner-not-in-cite 1, stale 1, low-overlap 1; AMI: ok 4, low-overlap 1, number-not-in-cite 2, polarity 1. AMI decision recall 0/9 for both prompts. Task A unchanged. Wall time per meeting 4.0 to 11.0 s (agenda-aware); peak server working set 7.3 GiB, GPU 7.3 GB total; the run took about 1 minute.

Reading: tightening cut hallucination (38% to 28%) and bad citations (6 to 4 of 25) mostly by making the model return fewer facts, and it lowered strict decision recall. The comma-list failure remains in 4 facts. The result is mixed, not an improvement in recall.

## Fix round 2 (owner authorized, 2026-11-25): diagnosis, citation re-anchoring, one prompt change, 14B confirmation

Diagnosis (6 invented meetings, 7B; failing facts dumped with their cited lines): of 18 failures, 7 unparsed citations, 6 numbers not in the cited lines, 4 low overlap, 1 polarity. The inspected cases were the model citing lines that do not hold its claim (line-number drift inside a 3,000-word chunk) and compound facts bundling several numbers.

Changes: (1) `reanchor`: when a fact's own citation fails for a citation-shaped cause, search the chunk for the 1 to 3 lines that contain every number, date and owner in the fact with stem overlap of at least 0.3, and use them; otherwise leave the fact unsupported. Results report model-cited and re-anchored citations separately. (2) Prompt v3 (hash `6ed489b881790279`): no `owner` field (the name goes in the text), one claim per fact, clearer decision and date definitions. 71 outline model-test tests pass; ESLint and the register check are clean.

Stop rule from the plan for this round: continue to a private campaign only at 60% decision recall and under 15% hallucination. Hallucination now passes on invented data (13% on the 7B, 10% on the 14B; the product bar is 5%), but decision recall does not (39% best, on the 14B; 6% on the 7B), and AMI decisions are not recovered by any configuration. **The stop rule is not met.** The limit is extraction recall: the models return too few decisions, and on real conversational speech none of the annotated decisions. Candidate designs for a different approach: a two-pass extract-then-classify (first list candidate statements per chunk by line, then assign topic and kind), or sentence-level candidate pre-selection with the model only classifying and rewriting.
