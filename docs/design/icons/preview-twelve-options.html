Scribe icon options: twelve designs

Options for the owner to choose from; none is shipped. White glyph on the indigo tile. Each shown at 220 px, then 16, 32 and 64 px, on light and dark backgrounds.

H. Bars, a gap and a written line (larger nib and scribble)

H-1: three bars, a gap, a bold scribble led by a large nib

H-2: four bars rising into a bold scribble, stylus writing

H-3: heavy bars, a gap, a looping scribble led by a large nib

J. Sound curves, then a scribble written by a pen

J-1: sound curves, then a scribble written by a fountain-pen nib

J-2: three sound curves, a scribble, a sleek stylus

J-3: sound curves and a longer scribble, steeper nib with grip

W. Waveform next to a text cursor

W-1: four sound bars, then an I-beam text cursor

W-2: three sound bars, a caret, a short written line

W-3: five symmetrical bars, then an I-beam

C. Sound curves next to a text cursor

C-1: three sound curves, then an I-beam text cursor

C-2: two big sound curves, a caret, a short written line

C-3: three sound curves flowing into a thick caret
