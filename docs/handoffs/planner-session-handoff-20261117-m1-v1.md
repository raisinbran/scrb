## 1. Owner-approved goals, scope, and operating decisions

### M1: five actions on the same recording
1. Record separate, synchronized **microphone** and **meeting/remote-audio** tracks with actual non-silent intended content.
2. Show live text with provisional speaker grouping, including **You** and two distinguishable remote speakers from the same meeting track. Confirm words against known turns, not only colored/source labels.
3. Stop normally and obtain a saved final transcript with correctly assigned speaker labels; sample at least three known nonoverlapping turns per remote speaker, and check microphone **You**.
4. Click transcript text/timestamps to seek and play the corresponding audio at approximately the correct time (about 3 seconds tolerance); verify long-turn follow-along separately.
5. Copy **the entire saved final transcript**, including virtualized/offscreen rows, with correct order, speaker labels, and corrected text; verify actual clipboard contents, not a toast.

One passing invented/public conversation shows *functional* M1 integration; it does **not** automatically close the separate **real-meeting speaker-reliability** concern. The owner must accept both. A planned test expected to exceed 20 minutes total, including processing, requires explicit approval; preserve any recording still processing instead of killing it on a timer. Only previously approved specific longer tests are authorized.
