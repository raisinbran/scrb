## 2. M1 — exact acceptance contract and most recent evidence

Owner's five required steps, on **one known source/build and genuine two-track recording**:

1. Save **separate non-silent microphone and meeting/whole-computer audio** tracks, with integrity/gap evidence and intended source attribution.
2. Show **live text grouped by provisional voices**, including two distinguishable remote people through the **same meeting-audio track** (not just `You` versus `Meeting`). A synthetic 3–4-minute run can demonstrate functional integration; **overall speaker reliability** additionally requires representative real-meeting evidence. M1 is not accepted from model-only replay, seeded transcripts, mock screens or count labels.
